# The potential impact of reductions in international donor funding on tuberculosis in low- and middle-income countries

This GitHub repository contains the scripts and files used to generate results for:

**1. Health_Impact:** "_Clark RA, McQuaid CF, Richards AS, et al. The potential impact of reductions in international donor funding on tuberculosis in low- and middle-income countries. The Lancet Global Health, Volume 13, Issue 9, e1517 - e1524"_.
   
**2. Household_Economic_Burden:** "_Portnoy A, Clark RA, Jit M, et al. The potential impact of reduced international donor funding on the household economic burden of tuberculosis in low- and middle-income countries."_
